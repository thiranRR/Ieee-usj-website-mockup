## STRICT BRAND GUIDELINES (IEEE SB USJ)
The website must strictly adhere to a light, minimal theme. Dark mode is prohibited.
- **Background Color:** `#fdfdfd` (Use Tailwind `bg-[#fdfdfd]` or `bg-white`).
- **Primary Accent (IEEE SB Blue):** `#086b9e`. Use for buttons, primary highlights, and active states.
- **Typography:** Montserrat font family. Do NOT use Calibri or script fonts.
- **Primary Text:** `#000000` (Tailwind `text-black`).
- **Secondary/Paragraph Text:** `#68686c` (Tailwind `text-[#68686c]`).
